The Framework

The Premonition Matrix™

Every brand is evaluated across five categories. Each is scored 0–100, then weighted into a single overall reading and a tier. At signup you choose the category that matters most to you — and Premonition weights your analysis toward it.

01

Influence

How visible, trusted, and impactful is this brand in the public arena?

How a brand shows up in the world and how effectively it shapes public perception.

02

Identity

Does this brand have a clear, compelling, and differentiated sense of self?

The internal DNA of the brand — the story it tells and the values it stands on.

03

Infrastructure

Is this brand structurally strong enough to support growth, influence, and trust?

The operational backbone that supports the brand's promises.

04

Intelligence

How well does this brand perceive the landscape, and how prepared is it for what's coming?

The analytical and predictive engine of the brand — the part that sees ahead.

05

Impact

Is this brand creating tangible, positive results that validate its strategy?

The real-world results of the brand's actions.

The Output

A diagnostic, not a dashboard

Every analysis returns the same seven sections, so you can compare this month against last month rather than starting over each time.

Report

Score breakdown

All five Matrix categories scored 0–100, with the weight each one carried in your overall reading and how much it contributed.

Report

Crisis signals

Named risk flags with severity and the specific evidence that raised each one — narrative incoherence, promise/delivery gaps, identity drift, unconverted effort.

Report

Trajectory & forecast

Where the narrative is heading. On your first run this is read structurally; from your second run onward it is a real period-over-period comparison.

Report

30-day alignment plan

Four weeks of specific actions aimed at your weakest categories, plus one week of consolidation. Concrete verbs, not principles.

Report

Sector benchmark

Where you sit against other analysed brands in your sector — per category, by percentile. Shown once there are enough peers to mean something.

Report

Confidence rating

How much the supplied evidence actually justifies the scores. Thin intake produces a lower confidence rating rather than a confident guess.
